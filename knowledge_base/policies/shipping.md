# Shipping Policy

## Shipping Options & Rates
We offer standard and expedited shipping options for domestic and international deliveries.

- **Standard Ground Shipping:** 3 to 5 business days ($5.99 flat rate, FREE on orders over $50).
- **Expedited Express Shipping:** 1 to 2 business days ($14.99 flat rate).
- **International Shipping:** 7 to 14 business days (calculated at checkout based on destination and customs duty).

## Processing Times
- Orders placed Monday through Friday before 2:00 PM EST are processed on the same business day.
- Orders placed after 2:00 PM EST, on weekends, or on public holidays will be processed on the following business day.

## Order Tracking
Once your package ships, an automated email and SMS containing a tracking link will be sent to you. You can also view live tracking updates on your order details page.

## Lost or Stolen Packages
If your tracking status indicates "Delivered" but you have not received your package:
1. Check around your delivery location, mailbox, or with neighbors/building reception.
2. Wait 24 hours as carrier updates can occasionally precede final delivery.
3. If the package is still missing after 24 hours, contact customer support to open a carrier trace investigation.
