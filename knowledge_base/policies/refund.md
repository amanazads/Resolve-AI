# Refund Policy

## Refund Eligibility
Customers may request a full refund within **30 days** of receiving their order.

To be eligible for a refund:
1. The product must be returned in its original condition and packaging.
2. All original accessories, cables, manuals, and protective packaging must be included.
3. Proof of purchase (Order ID or receipt) must be provided.

## Non-Refundable Items
- Custom engraved or personalized items.
- Products marked as "Final Sale" or promotional clearance items.
- Items damaged due to customer misuse, liquid damage, or unapproved modification.

## Refund Process & Timeline
1. **Initiation:** Submit a refund request via your online account dashboard or contact customer support.
2. **Return Shipping:** Once approved, we will issue a prepaid return shipping label via email.
3. **Inspection:** Upon arrival at our warehouse, items undergo inspection within 2-3 business days.
4. **Credit:** Once inspected and approved, refunds are credited back to your original payment method within 5-7 business days depending on your financial institution.

## Partial Refunds
If returned items are missing accessories or display minor wear, a partial refund or re-stocking fee of 15% may apply.
