# Warranty Policy

## Limited Hardware Warranty
All hardware products purchased directly from our official store come with a standard **1-Year Limited Manufacturer Warranty** starting from the original purchase date.

## What is Covered
- Defective materials or craftsmanship.
- Battery failures resulting in less than 50% capacity within the first year.
- Internal component failures during normal intended usage.

## What is NOT Covered
- Accidental drops, physical impact damage, or cracked screens.
- Water damage exceeding rated IP/5ATM specifications.
- Unauthorized disassembly, repair, or third-party modifications.
- Cosmetic wear and tear (scratches, dented cases).

## Claiming Warranty Service
To initiate a warranty claim:
1. Contact customer support with your proof of purchase and a description of the defect.
2. Provide photos or video demonstrating the issue if requested.
3. If approved, we will provide a repair, replacement unit, or refurbished unit of equal value.
