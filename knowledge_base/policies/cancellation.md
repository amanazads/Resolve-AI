# Order Cancellation Policy

## Cancellation Window
Orders can be cancelled free of charge within **24 hours** of placing the order, provided the order has not already entered the shipping/fulfillment stage.

## How to Cancel an Order
1. Provide your Order ID (e.g., ORD123) to our AI Support Assistant or go to your account dashboard under "My Orders".
2. If your order status is "Processing" or "Pending", you can request instant automated cancellation.
3. A full refund will immediately be initiated back to your original payment method.

## Orders Already Shipped
If more than 24 hours have passed or your order status is marked as "Shipped":
- The order cannot be cancelled in transit.
- Once the package arrives, you can refuse delivery or initiate a standard return under our 30-day Refund Policy.
