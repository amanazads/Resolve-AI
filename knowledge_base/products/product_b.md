# SmartFit Watch Ultra (Product B)

## Overview
The SmartFit Watch Ultra is a state-of-the-art smartwatch designed for health monitoring, fitness tracking, and seamless smartphone notifications.

## Key Specifications
- **Model Name:** SmartFit Watch Ultra (Product B)
- **Price:** $149.99
- **Display:** 1.85-inch Always-On AMOLED Touchscreen (454x454 resolution).
- **Battery Life:** Up to 7 days normal usage, 14 days in power-saver mode.
- **Sensors:** Heart rate monitor, SpO2 blood oxygen, sleep tracker, accelerometer, GPS + GLONASS.
- **Water Resistance:** 5 ATM (water-resistant up to 50 meters, suitable for swimming).
- **Strap Width:** 22mm interchangeable silicone straps.

## Features
- Over 100+ workout and sport modes.
- Real-time GPS tracking for outdoor runs and cycling.
- Heart rate spike alerts and stress monitoring.
- Bluetooth call receiving and music control playback.

## Compatibility
Requires SmartFit Companion App, available on iOS (version 14.0+) and Android (version 8.0+).
