# TechPro Wireless Noise-Canceling Headphones (Product A)

## Overview
The TechPro Wireless Noise-Canceling Headphones are our flagship audio product designed for audiophiles and working professionals. They feature active noise cancellation (ANC), premium memory foam ear cushions, and crystal-clear microphone performance for virtual calls.

## Key Specifications
- **Model Name:** TechPro ANC-900 (Product A)
- **Price:** $199.99
- **Battery Life:** Up to 30 hours playback with ANC enabled (40 hours with ANC turned off).
- **Charging:** USB-C fast charging (10 minutes charge gives 4 hours playback).
- **Connectivity:** Bluetooth 5.3 with multipoint connectivity (pair with up to 2 devices simultaneously).
- **Weight:** 250 grams.
- **Color Options:** Midnight Black, Silver Platinum, Navy Blue.

## Box Contents
- TechPro Headphones
- Hard-shell travel carrying case
- USB-C to USB-A charging cable
- 3.5mm audio cable for wired listening
- User guide & warranty document

## Compatibility
Fully compatible with iOS, Android, macOS, Windows, and Linux devices.
