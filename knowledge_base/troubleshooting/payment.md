# Payment Troubleshooting Guide

## Common Payment Issues & Solutions

### 1. Payment Declined / Transaction Failed
If your payment fails during checkout:
- **Card Details:** Verify card number, CVV, expiration date, and billing postal code match your bank's record.
- **Sufficient Funds:** Ensure adequate balance or available credit limit.
- **Bank Security Locks:** Many banks block online foreign or novel transactions. Contact your card issuer to approve the charge.

### 2. Double Charge Pending
If you see two identical pending charges on your bank statement:
- One is likely a temporary authorization hold placed during checkout.
- Pending holds automatically drop off within 3 to 5 business days without deducting funds.

### 3. Accepted Payment Methods
We accept Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover), PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Shop Pay.
