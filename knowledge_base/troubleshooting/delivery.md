# Delivery Troubleshooting Guide

## Shipment Delayed
If your package tracking has not updated for more than 48 hours:
- Weather events, holiday peak surges, or carrier delays can cause temporary pauses in tracking scans.
- If your package exceeds the estimated delivery date by 3 days, our system will automatically mark it for carrier inquiry.

## Wrong Address Delivered
If an order was shipped to an incorrect address entered at checkout:
- If the package has not yet shipped, contact support immediately to update shipping address.
- If the package is already in transit, carrier address redirection may incur a carrier rerouting fee.

## Damaged Delivered Package
If your item arrives physically damaged:
1. Take photos of the damaged external shipping box and the damaged product.
2. Keep all outer packaging materials.
3. Contact customer support within 48 hours of delivery to file a shipping damage claim for immediate replacement.
