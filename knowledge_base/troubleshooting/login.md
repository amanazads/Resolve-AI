# Account & Login Troubleshooting

## Password Reset
If you cannot sign in to your account:
1. Click **Forgot Password** on the login page.
2. Enter your registered email address.
3. Check your email inbox (and Spam/Junk folder) for a password reset link.
4. Click the link to set a new password (must be at least 8 characters long, containing a number and special character).

## Account Lockout
For security, accounts are temporarily locked for **15 minutes** after 5 consecutive failed login attempts. Please wait 15 minutes before retrying or use the password reset procedure.

## Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) Issues
If you lost access to your 2FA authenticator app:
- Use one of your emergency backup codes provided during setup.
- If backup codes are lost, contact support to initiate identity verification and 2FA reset.
