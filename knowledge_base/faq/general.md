# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## 1. What are your customer support operating hours?
Our AI Support Assistant is available 24/7. Human support representatives are available Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM EST.

## 2. Where is your company located?
Our corporate headquarters are located in San Francisco, California, with logistics fulfillment centers across North America and Europe.

## 3. How do I change my shipping address after placing an order?
You can request an address change within 1 hour of order placement by contacting support or selecting "Modify Address" under your recent orders list in the portal.

## 4. Do you offer student or military discounts?
Yes! We offer a 15% discount for verified students, active military personnel, and veterans. Verification is completed via ID.me at checkout.

## 5. What should I do if my item is out of stock?
You can sign up on the product page to receive an instant email notification as soon as inventory is replenished. Items usually restock within 1 to 2 weeks.
